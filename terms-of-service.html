KING VIDEO MANAGER — Terms of Service
Last updated: 2 November 2026

KING VIDEO MANAGER (KVM) is a private Windows desktop application used by its operator to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not a public upload service or a commercial service offered through this website.

AUTHORIZED CHANNEL MANAGEMENT
YouTube operations require Google OAuth 2.0 authorization for the intended channel. KVM checks the authenticated Channel ID. Uploads are initially private. Publishing at a future date requires an explicitly selected date and user confirmation. Playlist changes, metadata updates, thumbnails and comments must follow the authorized user's instructions. Comment pinning, where unavailable through the API, remains a manual YouTube Studio action.

CONTENT RESPONSIBILITY
The operator is responsible for reviewing scripts, media, titles, descriptions, credits and publication settings; holding the necessary rights; and making accurate audience and synthetic-content declarations. Production automation does not replace editorial review or copyright permissions.

GOOGLE AND YOUTUBE
KVM uses YouTube API Services. Users of its YouTube functionality are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms. Google processes data under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. KVM is independent of Google and YouTube and is not endorsed by them.

LOCAL APPLICATION AND SERVICE LIMITS
Production files and operation records are stored locally. External service availability, account permissions and API quotas can affect operations. An interrupted or uncertain operation must be reconciled before it is repeated. No uninterrupted service, Google approval or publication outcome is guaranteed.
