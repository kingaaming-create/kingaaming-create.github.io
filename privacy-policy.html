KING VIDEO MANAGER — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2 November 2026

KING VIDEO MANAGER (KVM) is a private Windows desktop application used by its operator to manage their own YouTube channels. This site describes the application and its data practices. Contact: kingaaming@gmail.com.

YOUTUBE AND GOOGLE SERVICES
KVM uses YouTube API Services and Google OAuth 2.0. YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

DATA AND PURPOSES
With the channel owner's consent, KVM accesses channel identity, video metadata and publication status, playlists and membership, and information needed for uploads. Requested features may also access comments and, through a separate optional read-only authorization, YouTube Analytics reports. These data support the owner's channel management: private uploads, approved metadata and playlist operations, requested comments, thumbnails and explicitly confirmed future publication. Each channel is checked against its own authenticated Channel ID.

LOCAL STORAGE AND PROTECTION
Channel configuration, scripts, media references, operation journals, receipts, reports and backups are stored on the operator's computer. OAuth tokens are stored separately for each channel and protected with Windows DPAPI. KVM sends authorized requests and user-selected upload content to Google/YouTube; this public information site does not provide access to those local files or credentials. Local voice and preparation services process the production inputs selected by the operator. Texts or kits shared manually with ChatGPT or Claude are shared by the operator, not automatically through the YouTube authorization.

LIMITED USE
KVM does not sell Google user data or provide it to advertisers. Google/YouTube data are used for the disclosed channel-management functions, not to train generalized AI models. Use of Google API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy.

CONTROL, REVOCATION AND DELETION
The owner chooses the channels and actions. Initial uploads are private; public scheduling requires an explicit future date and confirmation. Access can be revoked at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not itself delete videos already stored on YouTube.

To request deletion of locally stored authorized data, contact kingaaming@gmail.com. The operator handles deletion requests as soon as possible and within seven calendar days, including relevant local copies and backups. Retained YouTube API data must be refreshed or deleted according to applicable YouTube API retention rules. Deletion is an operator-managed process; KVM does not claim that revocation automatically purges the entire local media archive. User-created scripts and original media are distinct from data obtained through the YouTube API. Deleting local records does not delete content on YouTube; use YouTube Studio for that purpose.

SERVICE POLICIES
Google and YouTube process requests under their own policies. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page. For questions or deletion requests: kingaaming@gmail.com.
